== Stage: loop filter (luma + chroma) ========================================================
Clip: all-intra 1920x1080, 120 frames, libx264 -preset veryfast -g 1 -crf 38 (bS 4 on macroblock
edges, bS 3 inside), REAL content (mandelbrot). Decode with and without the loop filter:
  ffmpeg -benchmark -threads 1 -i intra.mp4 -f null -                          utime 0.780 0.789 0.786 s
  ffmpeg -benchmark -threads 1 -skip_loop_filter all -i intra.mp4 -f null -    utime 0.498 0.499 0.496 s
Difference (median) 0.288 s over 120 pictures x 8160 macroblocks = 294 ns per macroblock for luma
and chroma together. CAVEAT for any comparison: this includes ffmpeg's bS derivation and its
per-line/per-edge early exits on real content (many lines fail the 8.7.2.2 thresholds), whereas
our BenchmarkDeblockFrame is a flat-plane sweep of the kernels only, where every line runs the
whole formula and bS is given. The two are not the same workload; the 1.4x figure is indicative.
